# H2 measurements (2026-11-04)

Board ES3C35P, ESP-IDF 5.5.5, LVGL 9.6.0, QSPI 80 MHz, plan B rotation (rotate while copying).
Benchmark: `lv_demo_benchmark`, 480x320, `tools/bench_matrix.sh`. Baseline = 32-row strips in
internal SRAM, cache 32 KB I / 64 KB D, 1 draw unit.

## Memory (bytes, no Wi-Fi/BLE/audio yet)

| Phase | Internal free | Internal min | Largest block | PSRAM free |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| boot | 248 627 | 216 596 | 180 224 | 8 386 192 |
| ui-steady (LVGL with FreeRTOS) | 167 231 | 135 200 | 122 880 | 8 385 048 |

`ui` task stack: 4 896 B free of 8 192. Enabling `CONFIG_LV_OS_FREERTOS` cost about 9 KB of
internal SRAM (176 455 -> 167 231 free).

## Chunked transmit (current default)

The 80-row strip lives in PSRAM; `flush_cb` cuts it into column slices that fit one of two
16 KB DMA buffers, rotating one slice while the DMA sends the other. Internal SRAM free with
the UI up: 195 967 B (167 231 B before), PSRAM free 8 308 244 B. Full-screen flush 10.08 ms
at 80 MHz (20 transactions of 24 rows). `Empty screen` drops from 49 to 44 FPS because of the
extra CASET/RASET per slice.
